Green Ledger & sustainability

What is the Green Ledger?

The Green Ledger is our transparent project reporting framework that documents operational metrics such as rendering activity, energy usage, network transfer information, and project lifecycle data in alignment with ISO 14067 system boundaries.

What is ISO 14067?

ISO 14067 is an international standard for calculating and communicating the carbon footprint of products. ESSAR INC. structures its Green Ledger using these system boundaries to support transparent reporting.

How does ESSAR INC. support ESG initiatives?

We help organizations build environmentally conscious media workflows by reducing unnecessary travel, utilizing cloud-based production, maintaining transparent operational records, and providing sustainability documentation for projects.

What are Scope 3 emissions?

Scope 3 emissions are indirect emissions generated throughout a company's value chain, including transportation, travel, logistics, and outsourced services. Our production methodology is designed to reduce these operational impacts.
